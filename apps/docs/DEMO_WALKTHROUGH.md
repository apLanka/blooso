# Blooso Demo Walkthrough

## Overview

Blooso is a booking platform for beauty and wellness businesses. This guide walks through the key features.

## 1. Business Owner Flow

### Onboarding
1. Register at `/register`
2. Complete onboarding wizard: business info → location → hours
3. Land on dashboard

### Service Management
1. Go to **Services** in sidebar
2. Add categories (e.g. Hair, Nails)
3. Add services with duration, price, buffers

### Staff Management
1. Go to **Staff**
2. Add staff members with email
3. Assign services to staff
4. Set weekly schedules

### Calendar
1. Go to **Calendar**
2. View appointments by day
3. Create walk-in appointments
4. Update status (pending → confirmed → in progress → completed)
5. Copy review link for completed appointments

### Clients
1. Go to **Clients**
2. Add clients manually or they auto-create on booking
3. View client history, notes, tags

### Reviews
1. Go to **Reviews**
2. Reply to customer reviews
3. Filter by rating

### Dashboard
1. View KPIs: today's revenue, bookings, clients, avg rating
2. Revenue and bookings charts
3. Today's schedule
4. Top services

## 2. Customer Flow

### Search
1. Visit `/search`
2. Search by name or filter by category
3. Click **Book now** on a business

### Booking
1. Select service(s)
2. Select staff (or any)
3. Pick date and time slot
4. Enter guest info or log in
5. Complete payment (Stripe) or pay in person

### Review
1. After appointment, visit review link from email or calendar
2. Rate 1–5 stars and add comment
3. Submit
